What does it mean to be AI-native?

Every role at BIXIE requires an AI-native mindset — no exceptions. We're not looking for people who have "heard of ChatGPT". We're looking for people for whom AI is a second native language.

AI in daily work

You use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other tools daily — for analysis, writing, planning and code. AI is an extension of your brain.

Prompt engineering

You write prompts that deliver exact results — not generic text. You understand context, constraints and iteration.

Automation

You automate your own tasks before doing them manually. If you've done something twice — you already have an idea how to automate it.

Fast learning

You master new tools and frameworks in days, not months. AI helps you learn 10x faster and apply it immediately.

What does the application process look like?

Fast and transparent — no long processes. The whole process takes 1–2 weeks.

1

Application

Send your CV and a short letter to hello@bixie.ba. Tell us which AI tool you use daily and how it changes the way you work.

2

Short chat

30-minute video call — we get to know each other, you talk about your experience, we talk about the role and expectations.

3

Practical AI task

A realistic task related to the role — you solve it with AI tools, of course. We assess the result and your way of working.

4

Offer

If we're a match — offer within 48h and a welcome to the team. If not, you get constructive feedback.
