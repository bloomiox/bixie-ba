Open Source Models We Support

Meta — Llama 4

The most popular open source model family. Llama 4 Scout, Maverick and Behemoth cover ranges from lightweight to the most powerful models. Excellent for fine-tuning on specific domains.

Best for: General AI tasks, fine-tuning, employee assistants, document processing

Parameters: 8B – 2T | Context: 128K – 1M tokens | License: Llama 4 Community

DeepSeek — R1 / V3

Models that revolutionized the AI industry. DeepSeek R1 offers step-by-step reasoning comparable to o3, while V3 provides excellent general performance at extremely low cost.

Best for: Logical reasoning, mathematics, programming, cost-effective operations

Parameters: 7B – 671B | Context: 128K | License: MIT

Mistral AI — Large / Small

Leading European open source models based in France. Mistral Large competes with GPT-4, while Mistral Small is optimized for speed. Fully GDPR compliant.

Best for: GDPR-sensitive applications, multilingual apps, EU companies

Parameters: 7B – 123B | Context: 32K – 128K | License: Apache 2.0 / Mistral Research

Alibaba — Qwen 3

Qwen 3 excels at long context understanding, multilingual tasks and tool-use capabilities. Some models have up to 1M token context windows.

Best for: Long document processing, tool-use agents, multilingual tasks

Parameters: 0.5B – 235B | Context: 32K – 1M | License: Apache 2.0 / Qwen

Microsoft — Phi-4

Microsoft's family of small but powerful models. Phi-4 and Phi-4-mini offer impressive performance for their size, ideal for edge devices and local hosting.

Best for: Edge deployment, mobile devices, local hosting on modest hardware

Parameters: 3.8B – 14B | Context: 128K | License: MIT

Google — Gemma 3

Google's open source model family based on Gemini technology. Gemma 3 offers an excellent balance of size and performance with multilingual support.

Best for: Multilingual applications, fine-tuning, research and development

Parameters: 2B – 27B | Context: 8K – 128K | License: Gemma
